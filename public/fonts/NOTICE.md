`scripts/subset-field-font.py`, and `scripts/subset-code-font.py`; see
[../docs/style.md](../docs/style.md). Smiley Sans ships as
`SmileySans-Oblique` upstream. `FieldSans.woff2` is a renamed, normalized
derivative generated from Noto Sans KR and Noto Sans; its 126 glyphs cover
Latin Extended, Cyrillic, CJK, kana, and Hangul in one family.

design and are no longer referenced by the stylesheets.
